      First Visit Expectations

      Your First Visit

      What to Expect: Your Comfort Is Our Priority

      Your first visit is all about getting to know you and your dental needs. We’ve designed the process to be as comfortable and straightforward as possible, with every step focused on your health and confidence.

      Step 1 of 5

      01

      Welcome & Health History

      We'll start by getting to know you. Our friendly team will ask about your medical and dental history to understand your overall health. To make this part easy, please bring a list of any medications, vitamins, and supplements you are currently taking, along with their dosages. We encourage you to ask any questions you may have.

      02

      Diagnostic X-Rays

      Digital X-rays allow us to see what's happening beneath the surface of your teeth and gums — detecting issues like cavities between teeth, bone loss, and other concerns that aren't visible during a visual exam. Our digital X-rays are fast, comfortable, and emit significantly less radiation than traditional film.

      03

      Comprehensive Oral Examination

      Our doctor will perform a thorough examination of your teeth, gums, and surrounding tissues. This includes an oral cancer screening and an evaluation of your bite and jaw. We use the latest technology to ensure nothing is missed, giving you the most accurate picture of your oral health.

      04

      Personalized Treatment Planning

      After your exam, our doctor will walk you through the findings and discuss any recommended treatment in plain language — no jargon, no pressure. We'll outline your options and prioritize your care based on what's most important for your long-term health, so you can make informed decisions with confidence.

      05

      Cleaning, Questions & Next Steps

      If your gums are healthy, your first visit will include a professional teeth cleaning to remove plaque and tartar buildup and leave your smile fresh. Before you leave, our team will make sure all of your questions are answered, help you schedule any follow-up appointments, and review your insurance benefits or financing options.

      Smile Makeover Patients

      Complimentary Services,
      No Commitment Required

      For patients exploring smile makeover options, we offer two complimentary first-visit services designed to help you make the most informed decision possible — completely free of charge and with zero pressure.

      Get a Free Consultation & 3D X-Ray for Your Smile Makeover

      At Kai Dental, we understand that a smile makeover is a big decision. That’s why we offer a FREE Consultation and 3D X-ray for prospective smile makeover patients — so you can move forward with complete confidence.

      Why do I need a consultation and 3D X-ray?

      Before beginning your smile makeover, it’s important to have a thorough evaluation to determine the best course of action for your specific needs. Our consultation and 3D X-ray allow us to:

      • Evaluate the condition of your teeth and gums
      • Determine the quality and quantity of bone in your jaw
      • Create a personalized treatment plan
      • Answer any questions or concerns you may have

      How it works

      Getting started is easy. Simply schedule a FREE Consultation and we’ll conduct a comprehensive evaluation using our advanced 3D X-ray technology. We’ll then discuss your options and create a customized plan to help you achieve the healthy, confident smile you’ve always wanted.

      If you’re considering a smile makeover, take advantage of our FREE Consultation and 3D X-ray offer. Contact Kai Dental today to schedule your appointment and get started on the path to a beautiful smile!
