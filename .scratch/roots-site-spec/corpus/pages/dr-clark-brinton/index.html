Dr. Clark Brinton, DMD

Dr. Clark Brinton, a general dentist, has focused his career on dental implant treatments. His clinical practice is limited to dental implants, and he teaches other dentists about dental implants locally and nationally. Dr. Brinton is implant faculty for Dental Success Network, a National Educator for Neodent/Straumann Group and regularly invited to speak nationally at symposiums on dental implant treatment and emerging technologies.

Dr. Brinton, graduated as the class valedictorian from Oregon Health & Science University School of Dentistry in 2013. He served four years in the National Health Service Corps across rural New Mexico, where he honed his oral surgery skills and moonlighted providing IV sedation, third molar, and dental implant services. Returning to Oregon in 2017, Dr. Brinton began teaching other dentists how to place and restore dental implants. You can see Dr. Brinton’s ongoing teaching schedule here.

Dr. Brinton co-owns Element Dental Lab to meet the growing and complex implant restorative needs inside and outside his practice. He continues as an unrecovered CE addict, constantly attending and teaching implant courses. Dr. Brinton speaks nationally and is regularly invited to address the largest implant audiences in the United States. Dr. Brinton is one of the few implant dentists in the state of Oregon who is qualified to place zygomatic implants for those who don’t have enough bone in their upper jaw for regular dental implants. Dr. Brinton is the proud father of two daughters and one son.
